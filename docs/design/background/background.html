Background work in Summary

One block in the Summary column for what this conversation left running and what it ran: its background commands and its subagents. It has no panel of its own: it's a block like Tools or Plan.

Use the controls to step through the states. Hover a running row to see Stop, and click a command to open its terminal tab.

What each part is

  • Head. "Background · 2 running · 3 finished", with a chevron to fold it. It's open while something runs. Your choice is remembered per conversation, as Changes is.
  • Rows. The same glyphs as the subagent rows in the transcript. A command shows in monospace, with its address as a link. A subagent shows its title and its plain-language line. The time ticks each second on the right. On hover, Stop replaces the time.
  • Finished. Folded, newest first, five then "and N more". Each row says how it ended: "exited 0", "exit 1", "stopped", "done · $0.03".
  • Where it sits. Always the last block, under Plan and above the footer, whether something runs or not, so the blocks above never move. With nothing to show, there's no block.
  • Column closed. A count on the Summary icon. The column doesn't open by itself.
  • A chat has no commands, so it lists its subagents only, with no subheadings.
