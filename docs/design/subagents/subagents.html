Subagents, three directions

Antigravity shows a subagent as one quiet line that turns into a check, and opens it beside the conversation where you can talk to it. Piopio shows the same subagent in three places, and none of them is the answer. Below: what's wrong today, three ways to fix it, and a pick. Each mockup has a timeline switch on top (Working · One done · All done) so you can see it move. The findings and priorities in the mockups are made up to fill the screens; they aren't an audit of Piopio.

What's wrong today

  • One subagent, three places. A "Subagent … started · its result comes back here · Open" card in the transcript, a "Subagents · 3 running" block above the composer, and later a "Result from … · finished" row. The card never changes: it says "started" after the work is done.
  • Noise where the signal should be. Titles in monospace, the same trailing sentence on every card, and the live line is a raw shell command (bash cd /Users/…/piopio && sed -n '400,460p'…).
  • Open leaves the conversation. It replaces the page with the child session, and the child isn't in the sidebar, so the way back is the parent's row.
  • You can't talk to it from here. Steering a subagent means leaving the conversation that started it.

Rules all three share

  • One row per subagent, and it's live. The row Piopio's call creates is the subagent: its glyph moves from a ring to a check (or red), its grey line is what it's doing, then what it found. The "Result from" rows and the block above the composer go away.
  • Plain-language activity. The engine writes the line from the tool call, not its raw text: "Reading SessionView.swift", "Searching for Keychain", "Running the tests". Raw commands stay one click away.
  • Sans titles, no repeated sentences. "Its result comes back here" is said once, in the tooltip.
  • Queued is visible: past three, the fourth shows a dashed glyph and "starts when one finishes".

A · Beside

The Antigravity shape, in Piopio's chrome. Clicking a row opens the subagent in the right column, the same column Summary, Changes and Files use, with one tab per subagent. You see its brief, its steps as they happen and its report, and a box at the bottom steers it ("also check the OAuth callback"). Open in full moves it to the main area if you want the whole transcript. The conversation never leaves the screen.

B · One card

No side column. The subagents a turn starts are one card in the transcript. While any of them works the card is docked above the composer, so it's always in view, the way the "Subagents" block is today. When the last one finishes it settles back into its place in the transcript and Piopio's answer follows right under it. A row opens in place: its last steps while it works, its report when it's done, and a small "Tell it…" field. It's the cheapest to build and works in a narrow window.

C · Findings as they come

The new idea. What you want from a subagent isn't its activity, it's what it found. Read-only subagents get a finding tool (a title, a severity, evidence as file:line, a short detail) and report as they go. The card shows a lane per subagent (its count and newest finding) and one list of findings, merged and sorted by severity. You steer by reacting, not by typing: Dig deeper sends that finding back to its subagent, Not relevant tells it to drop that thread. Piopio's answer then cites findings (S2), and each citation opens its evidence. Hover a finding to see the actions.

Seeing what they're doing. Each lane's grey line is what that subagent is doing now ("Reading oauth.ts"). Click a lane and it opens beside, as in A: its brief, its steps as they happen with its findings at the moment it reported them ("Found: … server.ts:88"), and its own box. The findings list filters to that subagent. So C is A's column with findings on top, not a replacement for it.

C with a comparison: the findings make a table

An audit has an obvious order (high first). Most work doesn't. Take a question from spec 33: where should my Piopio Server run? Three subagents each research one place: a rented VPS, a PaaS like Fly.io, and the Mac mini at home. What each one finds isn't more or less urgent. It's a fact about one option on one aspect: "Fly · Always on: only on a paid machine; free ones sleep". So the card isn't a sorted list. It's a table that fills in while they work: one column per subagent (the lanes become the column heads) and one row per aspect. A cell shows a ring until its subagent gets there.

Steering changes shape with it:

  • Add a row: "+ Ask all of them" adds an aspect, and every subagent still working looks into it. Here: "Can it run Build?"
  • Drop a column: "Not an option" stops that subagent and greys its column. You've decided; it stops spending.
  • Check a cell: "Check" sends one fact back to its subagent to confirm against a second source.

Piopio's answer then cites cells (VPS · Cost), not findings by number. Hover a cell or a column head for its actions; click a column head to open that subagent beside.

Building different things, with roles

Subagents that change files aren't task subagents in Piopio. They're helpers on the board: each task gets its own branch and worktree, a Builder does it, QA checks it with fresh eyes, and the board merges it and runs the checks (the Development playbook). Roles here aren't parallel lanes. They're stages one task goes through. So the card changes again:

  • A row per task, with its stages: Builder → QA → Merge. Each stage is a glyph you can click to open that helper beside, as in A. A row's grey line is what the current stage is doing, and its diff grows live (+84 −3 · 4 files).
  • Sent back is a loop, not an event line. When QA sends a task back, the QA stage turns amber with its reason and the round ("round 2 of 3"), and the Builder stage spins again.
  • What a builder hands back is decisions. An audit finds things; a builder decides things you never asked about: "search covers messages, not tool output". Each one is a finding with a decision, listed under the tasks, with Keep or Change… (which tells that builder, or becomes a new task if it's done). You steer the build without reading the diff.
  • The plan block and the merge card become this card. It docks above the composer while tasks run (today's plan block), and ends with "Ready for main" and Merge into main in the same place.

The same row works for any playbook with roles in sequence: Writer → Editor, Researcher → Reviewer.

One tool, the shape follows what comes in

The two cases aren't two modes. The finding tool has a title, evidence and a detail, plus three optional fields, and the card picks its shape from what the subagents fill in:

The findings carryThe card showsExample
severityOne list, worst firstAn audit, a code review
option + aspectA table: options across, aspects downWhere to host, which library, which flight
verdict on a hypothesisEach hypothesis with its evidence, marked likely or ruled out"Why does the app freeze on big projects?": one subagent per suspect
decisionDecisions to Keep or Change, under the task rowsBuilders on the board, a writer drafting
none of themA plain feed, in the order they came"Collect what people say about X"

Piopio's brief to the subagents decides which fields they use (the playbook can set it: Research says option + aspect, Review says severity). Nothing in the UI asks you to choose.

Pick

A now, C as its next layer. A fixes all four problems and is what you liked: the row is live and quiet, and opening a subagent no longer takes you away. B is the fallback the column uses when the window is too narrow for it (the row opens in place instead). C is the part nobody else does. It changes what a subagent hands back, from an 8,000-character blob to findings Piopio can merge and cite, so the answer gets better, not only the UI. It fits read-only subagents, which is what task is for. A task with a single answer (trace this bug) has no findings and just looks like A.

Open questions

  • Talking to a subagent that finished. Its result already reached Piopio. Does a follow-up reopen it and send a second [Task] result, or stay between you and it? The guess: a second result, marked as an update.
  • Children in the sidebar? Antigravity doesn't list them; neither would A. Side conversations stay listed (24), subagents don't.
  • Claude Code / Codex tasks always wait and report no steps. In A their column shows the brief and "working on your account" until the result.
  • C's severity scale is for audits. Research would use "important / useful / aside". It may be the playbook's call, not the tool's.
