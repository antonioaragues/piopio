0aBusy sidebar and its rules
1
Only what's liveUnder each project: sessions that are running, waiting on you, or touched in the last 3 days. The rest fold into "9 earlier sessions".
2
Attention floats upWaiting (amber) and running items sort first inside their project, and also count in Needs you, so you never scroll to find them.
3
Caps per sectionEach section shows only its most recent few (here 2 projects, 2 notebooks, 1 agent, 1 chat), sized to fit the window. The header count ("Projects · 6") and "4 more ›" open the full list.
4
Pin and collapsePinned keeps any item at the top regardless of activity. Click a section header to collapse it; the state is remembered.
5
Search filters in placeTyping in Search narrows the sidebar across all sections, including folded items. ⌘K jumps anywhere.
6
Auto-archiveDone sessions and chats untouched for 30 days move to Archived in the index. Nothing is deleted.
