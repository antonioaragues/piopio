# Overview mockup (2026-11-22)

`Overview.html` is a review mockup for [spec 34](../../spec/34-overview.md), drawn in the grey style
(docs/design/grey tokens, light and dark, card layout). It is a reference, not a handoff: nothing here is built.
The numbers are made up but add up (the days sum to the month, the rows to the total).

- **A** · Spend: Piopio's estimate beside OpenRouter's bill (never added), on track for the month, plan use; by day
  (This Mac / Cloud); by what (regroups by model, provider, project, where); budgets; Cheaper next time.
- **B** · Setup: the map (you · Piopio on This Mac and in the cloud · what it uses), hover a node to see what it
  touches, the blue dot for what needs you, dashed for unused; then What runs where.
- **C** · Activity: a year of days, counts by area against the range before, Disk with cleanups.
- **D** · The way in: the sidebar footer's cost line, its hover summary, and a click to Spend.
