Project settings in a modal

A project lives in the sidebar. Clicking its row folds or unfolds its conversations, and nothing else. On hover, next to the + for a new conversation, a settings button opens a modal with everything about the project: folders, instructions and notes, permissions, who works here, routines. The project page goes away.

1 · The project row

Two buttons appear on hover (and stay on the project you're in): settings and +. They take the place of the branch name. A folded project shows a › after its name, as today. With a team working there, the project's first nested row is its Board.

The settings icon is sliders, not a gear: the gear in the sidebar's footer is the app's Settings. Right-click on a project also has "Project Settings…" and "New Conversation", for the keyboard and VoiceOver.

2 · The settings modal

A sheet over the window, in the order you'd look for things: what it uses (folders, instructions and notes), then how it works (permissions, who works here, routines). Changes apply as you make them, as in macOS Settings, so the only button is Done. Removing the project sits in the footer, in grey, away from Done.

With a team, "Who works here" shows the team with Change, and two rows join How it works: Tasks at once and Checks. Choosing "A team…" opens the team picker and its fit check, as today. Esc or Done closes; the modal is as tall as its content, and scrolls past the window's height.

For you to decide

  1. Losing the project page. With the row only folding, nothing would open the page, so I'd remove it. Its box is already the project's + page, and its conversations are in the sidebar. The alternative is keeping it behind a double click, but that's hidden. OK to remove it?
  2. The Board row. Without the page, a team's board needs a place; I'd put it first under its project in the sidebar. The alternative is a button in the modal, but the board is work in progress, not a setting.
  3. Instructions in the modal, or a line on the + page? I'd keep both: the modal for editing, and on a new project's + page the existing "Instructions… Write them" line, which would now open the modal.
