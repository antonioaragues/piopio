Temporary chats

A temporary chat is kept for 24 hours after your last message, then deleted, unless you keep it. While it lasts, other conversations can't search it and nothing from it goes to About me (spec 14 M4).

Round 2. You chose A for the chat itself. This round is about turning it on, the same way on Home and on the New conversation page that the compose button opens.

Chosen: A

  • In Conversations with the others, with a dashed bubble and the hours left.
  • Coming back to it, a strip under the toolbar says what happens to it, with Keep.
  • Before the first message, the draft row in the sidebar reads "Temporary chat" with the dashed bubble.
1

A button in the page's corner

A dashed bubble at the top right of Home and of New conversation. Click it and the page turns temporary: the bubble fills in, New conversation's heading reads "Temporary chat", and the line under the box says what that means. Click again to go back. Off the page, ⇧⌘N.

2

A switch above the box

A labelled switch, "Temporary", on the right just above the box: beside the date on Home, under the heading on New conversation. It says what it does without a tooltip, and it's right where you're about to type.

Home · off

New conversation · on

3

In the box's + menu

The + that adds files and folders gets "Temporary chat" with its shortcut. Nothing new on the page until you turn it on; then the line under the box says so, with Undo. The quietest of the three, and the hardest to find the first time.

The same in all three

  • Turned on, the line under the box reads: deleted 24 hours after your last message unless you keep it, not searched, nothing to About me, and the model's provider still receives what you send.
  • It stays on for that one chat only. The next New conversation starts normal again.
  • ⇧⌘N, and File › New Temporary Chat, start one from anywhere.
