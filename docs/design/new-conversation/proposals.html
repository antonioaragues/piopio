The New conversation page

Today it says "New conversation" and a sentence about how projects work, which is the same for every project. Three ways to make it feel like this project, using only what Piopio already knows: the first line of AGENTS.md, the latest lines of memory, the linked folders, the permissions and the recent conversations. Each one also covers the page with no project.

A

One question, centered

The quietest. The page asks "What's next in Tesis?" in the middle of the window, over a slightly larger box. No label and no description. Under the box, a row of small pills: the conversations to pick up and, after them, what this one will be given (linked folders, memory).

B

The project, at a glance

The page is about the project. The title is its name, with its path, team and permissions under it, and the first line of its instructions as the description ("A PhD thesis on soil microbiomes…"), so you see what every conversation is told. Under the box, two short columns: Pick up (latest conversations) and Lately in memory (its last lines).

C

An empty conversation

The page looks like the conversation it's about to become: the box sits at the bottom, where the conversation's composer is, so pressing Return doesn't move anything. The middle holds the project's mark, its name, its first line of instructions, and what this conversation gets (folders, memory, permissions). Recent conversations are small cards just above the box.

D

C, with a card that says where it goes and what it knows

From the Claude window we keep one idea: the choice of place sits above the box, in the middle, as a card, not as a chip you have to open. What's Piopio's is what the card holds. Along its top, the places as a quiet row (No project, then your latest projects). Below that, what this conversation will be given there: its instructions, its memory, its folders and what it may do, each a row you can click. The box stays at the bottom with only +, the model and send, because where and how already sit in the card.

Good
  • Where it goes and what it knows are read before you type, in plain sentences ("Told", "Remembers", "Can read", "May"), without opening anything.
  • Switching place is one click, and the card changes to show what that place brings: the honest version of Claude's folder list.
  • Like C, the box doesn't move when you send; after sending, the conversation's composer shows the place and permissions as today.
Costs
  • The place and permissions leave the box's bar on this page, so the box looks different here than in a conversation.
  • A card in the middle is more chrome than A or C; it earns it only because each row is real and clickable.
  • The places row fits about four projects; more go under Other….

My pick

C. It's the most "bonita" because it's the most honest: the screen is the empty conversation, the box doesn't move when you send, and the middle says in one line what this conversation will be given. It's also the one that best answers the question behind the linked folders and memory work: "what does Piopio know here?".

A if you want the calmest page and are fine with the context hidden in pills. B if projects should feel like places with their own identity, at the cost of a busier page.
