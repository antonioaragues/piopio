New, and Agents on their own

Three ways to do the two changes: every New lands on the same start page, where the project is something you pick (or don't), and Agents leave the sidebar lists to become a place of their own, next to Search and Home.

All three share: ⌘N, the compose button and a project's + open the same page (a project's + just preselects it). Projects keep their + (it makes a folder); Conversations lose theirs. New agent moves into the Agents page. Teams go with Agents, since both are definitions you set up once. Location (This Mac / Cloud) is drawn but greyed: it appears when the cloud engine exists (spec 18, CF7).

A

Agents next to Home, project as a chip

The smallest step from today. Agents become a fourth row under Search, Home and Needs you, and open one page with agents and teams. The start page is today's "What's on your mind?" box; the project picker is the first selector in its bottom row, and the line under the box says where the message goes.

C

Agents as their own place, where as a sentence

Agents get the whole sidebar. Clicking Agents slides the lists over to agents and teams, with a back row to your work, like Mail's folders or a drill-in. The start page reads as a sentence, "New conversation in no project on this Mac": each underlined word is a menu, and your three latest projects are one click below the box.

C2 · Inside Agents the sidebar is about agents: their list, teams and upcoming runs. The + in the top bar becomes "New agent" here; ‹ Agents goes back to your work.

Good
  • Agents have space to grow (runs, channels, contacts) without crowding projects.
  • The sentence says where the message goes and what it may do, which is spec 18's rule for phase 1, in plain words.
  • Quick picks make "in a project" one click for the usual three.
Costs
  • A swapping sidebar is a mode, which v2 removed on purpose; you can't see a project and an agent side by side in the sidebar.
  • The compose button means two things depending on where you are, which breaks "New always goes to the same page" for agents.

My pick

A, borrowing two things. It keeps spec 18's "start by writing", costs the least to build, and the sidebar ends up about your work only. From B, the list of recent projects with their permission next to each one, inside A's menu. From C, the quick picks under the box ("Or in piopio · BioGen · financial_ia"), so the usual project is one click without opening anything.

B is the better choice if you expect people to pick a project most of the time; C if agents will grow into their own product (runs, contacts, channels) and deserve a whole sidebar.
