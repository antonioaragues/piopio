One /learn

Project lessons (spec 14 M5), /learn (32) and skills from how you work (40 SH0) become one thing: Piopio looks back at what happened and proposes what to keep. Each thing goes where it belongs, and nothing is saved until you say so.

The example is a Build session in the piopio project where you corrected the agent twice and showed it how a release is made.

1 · Three ways in

You ask for it, you tell it exactly what to keep, or Piopio offers after you've corrected it. All three end in the same card. Decided 2026-11-24: design A, with its choices in the card.

a

/learn: look back at this conversation

  • Reads the conversation's corrections, what worked, and what you showed it, then proposes one card.
  • In chats too: there it can only propose skills and About me.
b

/learn with words: keep exactly this

  • Replaces spec 14's /remember: one command, not two.
  • Piopio only decides where it goes and how to word it; the card shows one item.
c

After you correct it, an offer

  • After a turn where you denied a command with a note or said "no, use…", one quiet line under the answer.
  • At most once per session; × hides it. Settings › Memory can turn offers off.

2 · The card answers itself

Design A, with its choices at the bottom of the card instead of in the composer's tray. A proposal doesn't stop the agent, so nothing waits on the composer: the tray stays for what does (plans, questions, approvals), as side-conversation offers already do.

a

Waiting for you

  • Grouped by where it goes, shown as it will be written. Each block shows a few lines; the rest opens in place (“2 more lines”, “4 steps”).
  • Not now and Edit… as text, Save in black: the view's one black button while the composer is empty.
  • Edit… opens one sheet to change, move or drop items. Saving part of it is done there.
b

You kept writing without answering

  • Sending another message folds the card to one line: “not saved”, with Review to open it again where it is.
  • Nothing is lost and it takes no room. It stays unsaved until you choose; it never saves by itself.
  • Not now folds it the same way; Dismiss (in Edit…, or the line's ×) removes it.

3 · Afterwards

Opening the skill before saving it, and what stays in the conversation once it's saved.

a

A skill, opened

  • New skill, or merged into one you have (then shown as a diff of that skill).
  • When it's used is the skill's description: what the model reads to decide to load it.
  • Saved where pi looks: ~/.pi/agent/skills/, or the project's .pi/skills/ if it's only for this project.
b

Saved: one line that stays

  • The card folds to one line with links to each place it wrote, and Undo for the whole save.
  • AGENTS.md shows in the session's Changes like any other edit: you commit it or not.
  • Skills list in Settings › Skills (PAR12) and in the Library.

Throughout

  • Nothing is written until Save. Undo on the saved line takes the whole save back.
  • Every item says why, in a few words ("you corrected this twice"), so you can judge it.
  • Secrets and personal details from the conversation are never proposed for AGENTS.md or a skill.
  • A temporary chat never offers and its /learn can't write About me.
